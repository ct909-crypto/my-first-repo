# Design Evaluation

A five-reviewer panel assessed the design: identification, measurement, theory–mechanism, feasibility/data, and journal skeptic. Reports are in `design/reviews/`. The initial panel raised 8 critical issues, and an independent fix-verifier checked the revised design in two rounds (`design/reviews/fix-verifier.md`). The final verdict is PASS on all five roles, with 0 unresolved critical or major issues.

Panel checks:
- **Claim strength:** descriptive (causal gate invoked; `design/causal-gate.md`).
- **Outcome–mechanism alignment:** prevalence-stock.
- **Journal fit:** ERSS. The paper is descriptive, puts its scope in the title and abstract, and maps each hypothesis to theory-based tests.
- **Hypothesis coverage:** every hypothesis H1–H5 has at least one model.
- **Power:** feasibility-limited. The trend MDES is 0.07–0.10 log points, and the blueprint discloses that H4 may be inconclusive.
