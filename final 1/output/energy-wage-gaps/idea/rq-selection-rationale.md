# RQ Selection Rationale

Four candidate questions were evaluated by five lenses (theorist, methodologist, domain expert, journal editor, devil's advocate).

**Selected: RQ1.** It answers all parts of the user's request (size of renewable/non-renewable gaps, persistence net of occupation, source-specific gaps, occupational heterogeneity, 2021-2025 trend) and it adjudicates a live disagreement in the literature: green-premium studies (Curtis and Marinescu 2023; Godoy and Isaksen 2025; IMF 2024; Kuai et al. 2025; Whittard et al. 2024) versus evidence that legacy/brown jobs pay as much or more (Godoy and Isaksen's brown premium; Colmer, Lyubich and Voorheis's conditional parity). RQ2 (composition decomposition) and RQ3 (dispersion) are folded into RQ1 as secondary analyses.

**Rejected: RQ4** has a fatal flaw: a causal labor-demand claim cannot be identified from eight industries with overlapping three-year OEWS sample panels.

**Feasibility.** Five annual files contain 2,096 detailed occupation-industry cells for NAICS 2211, of which 2,075 have published mean hourly wages; only two cells are top-coded. Geothermal (4-8 occupations per year) and biomass (12-14) are thin and will be reported with caution.

**Key risks carried forward.** No worker-level controls; regional composition unobserved; OEWS estimates pool three years of survey panels, smoothing trends; small cells. Claims stay descriptive.
