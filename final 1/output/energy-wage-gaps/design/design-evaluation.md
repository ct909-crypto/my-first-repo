# Design Evaluation

Five lenses reviewed the draft design. No critical issues were found.

- **Identification:** Claim strength is descriptive; fixed effects serve as descriptive adjustment rather than causal identification, so the causal gate is not triggered. Reviewers asked that the manuscript say plainly that within-occupation gaps may partly reflect worker sorting.
- **Measurement:** The reviewers recommended the May CPI-U deflator, explicit handling of top-coded (`#`) and suppressed (`*`) cells, and a median-wage robustness outcome.
- **Theory and mechanism:** Every hypothesis maps to a model. H3 needs source-specific indicators (M4), and H4 needs occupational groups fixed before estimation (M5).
- **Feasibility and data:** Geothermal and biomass cells are thin. Other generation (221118) is excluded from the main contrast.
- **Journal skeptic:** The design must take seriously BLS cautions about comparing OEWS estimates over time, and it must show robustness to treating nuclear as a separate group.

**Round 2.** After the scholar-causal audit, the identification reviewer confirmed that the fixed effects are a descriptive adjustment. Backdoor paths through worker sorting and region remain open, so the claim stays descriptive.

**Round 3.** We reviewed the naming alignment and the split into M8 and M9. Neither changes the substance of the design.
