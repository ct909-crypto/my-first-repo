**Highlights:**

- Same-occupation wages are about 11.5% lower in U.S. renewable than in fossil and nuclear generation.
- Occupational composition does not explain the renewable wage gap.
- Hydro pays like fossil fuel; solar, wind, geothermal and biomass pay less; nuclear pays more.
- Gaps are largest for plant operators and maintenance workers.
- The gap did not narrow from 2021 to 2025 as renewable employment doubled.
